Civic Privacy Charter & Implementation Commitments

Zero-Telemetry Commitment • தமிழ்நாடு குடிமக்கள் உறுதிமொழி
phonelink_lock

No Automatic SMS Inbox Access

We never request READ_SMS or notification listener permissions on your device.

Unlike commercial caller ID apps that harvest address books and mirror private communication streams, Namma Kavacham has zero capability to monitor your inbox automatically.

verified_userZero inbox or background reading permissions
touch_app

User-Controlled Input & No Background Monitoring

Triage occurs exclusively when you explicitly paste text, upload a screenshot, or capture an image.

No background monitoring daemon or continuous screen surveillance runs. Camera capture, when selected, stays isolated to user-triggered snapshots with immediate preview.

checkExplicit citizen-driven triage action only
memory

Ephemeral In-Memory Processing & Retention Scope

The MVP does not intentionally maintain a user-facing message archive. Processing and retention depend on configured services and infrastructure.

Submitted content is handled in volatile runtime memory during triage analysis. No commercial advertising profiles, message dossiers, or conversational training stores are created.

policyNo persistent citizen archive • Configured runtime isolation
fingerprint

No Sender Identity Verification Claim

Assessments evaluate observable message indicators, linguistic coercion, and link structures—never sender authenticity.

Because telecommunication SMS headers (e.g. VK-SBIINB) can be forged on spoofable networks, Namma Kavacham analyzes what the message asks you to do rather than validating sender identity.

infoIndicator-based evaluation, not telecom authentication

Advisory Nature & Citizen Responsibility

Namma Kavacham AI provides heuristic educational guidance based on observable indicators. It does not possess judicial authority or access to closed banking ledgers. If you have transferred money to a fraudulent account, report immediately to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
