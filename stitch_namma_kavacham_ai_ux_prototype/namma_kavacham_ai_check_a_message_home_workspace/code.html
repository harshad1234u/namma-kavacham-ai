What Namma Kavacham Checks & What We Explicitly Do Not

To prevent false senses of security, civic cyber tools must state their exact capabilities and strict ethical boundaries.

fact_check

What We Check

Active Deterministic Verifications
  • check_circle
    Urgent Threat & Coercive Language: Identifies panic triggers ("disconnected tonight", "account blocked", "arrest warrant").
  • check_circle
    Deceptive Payload & URL TLDs: Spots non-government domains pretending to be .gov.in or .nic.in, as well as disguised APK downloads.
  • check_circle
    Curated Static Schemes Knowledge Base: Compares claims directly against authentic procedural rules for PM-Kisan, TNEB, Aadhaar UIDAI, and Income Tax refunds.
  • check_circle
    OTP & PIN Demand Detection: Instantly raises flags whenever messages instruct users to forward 6-digit codes or scan unknown QR codes.
do_not_disturb_on

Explicit Product Boundaries

Strict Zero-Overreach Principles
  • cancel
    No Automatic SMS Inbox Snooping: We do not ask for Android notification listeners or background SMS inbox read privileges.
  • cancel
    No Sender Phone Number Certification: Caller ID and SMS headers can be spoofed by VoIP. We judge the message payload, never phone identities.
  • cancel
    No Hallucinatory Web RAG over Unverified Sources: Our models only benchmark against hardcoded, authoritative civic registries to eliminate AI false claims.
  • cancel
    No False Guarantees or Identity Certification: Attack vectors evolve daily. We do not certify senders or offer indemnification; assessments reflect observable heuristic cues for defensive citizen decision-making.

Curated State & National Safety Facts

Instant verification standards embedded inside our rule engine

Updated Weekly
Tamil Nadu Electricity Board (TNEB)

TANGEDCO never threatens same-night disconnection via personal SMS. Disconnection notices require physical written warnings. Official toll-free: 1912.

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Installments are directly credited via DBT to verified Aadhaar bank accounts. Government never asks for registration payment fees or processing deposits.

UIDAI / Aadhaar Updates

UIDAI never asks citizens to share OTP over the phone or install screen-sharing software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) to update biometric details.
